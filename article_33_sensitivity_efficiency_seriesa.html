How to prepare 18-36 month projections with sensitivity analysis

By SheBlooms 7 min read

At Series A, hand-wavy financial numbers do not pass. Investors expect detailed, sensitivity-tested projections and efficiency metrics like Magic Number and Burn Multiple.

The financial model at Series A needs to withstand a full data room audit. That means every number is traceable to an assumption, every assumption has been stress-tested across multiple scenarios, and the efficiency metrics investors use to compare you against peers are calculated and ready.

Build sensitivity into the model itself

Rather than a single projection, show a base case alongside at least one downside scenario, what happens if growth is slower, CAC rises, or churn increases. This is what "sensitivity analysis" means in practice, and its absence is one of the fastest ways to lose credibility at this stage.

1
State Magic Number and Burn Multiple
These efficiency metrics are the standard reference investors use to benchmark you against peers. Calculate and present them yourself rather than making the investor derive them.
2
Break down burn by category
Separate salaries from other operating expenses. Investors want to see whether burn growth is driven by headcount, infrastructure, or something else specifically.
3
Address exit strategy and next-round timing
Have a considered, honest view on M&A versus IPO possibilities, and when you expect to be ready for the next round given the current trajectory.
Will not pass a data room audit
  • A single base-case projection with no downside scenario
  • No Magic Number or Burn Multiple calculated
  • Burn shown as one blended number, no category breakdown
Passes a data room audit
  • Base case plus at least one stress-tested downside scenario
  • Magic Number and Burn Multiple stated clearly
  • Burn broken down by category (salaries vs. other opex)
A Series A-ready financial statement
"Magic Number: 0.9. Burn Multiple: 1.4. Base case reaches €1.2M ARR by month 24; downside case (25% slower growth, 15% higher CAC) still reaches €900K ARR without requiring an additional raise. 68% of burn is salaries."

"The first version of our model only showed the case where everything went right. Adding the downside scenario ourselves, before anyone asked for it, was what got us through diligence faster."

SheBlooms founder
What Series A investors check

18-36 month projections with at least one stress-tested downside scenario. Magic Number and Burn Multiple stated clearly. Burn broken down by category. A considered view on exit strategy and next-round timing.

Key takeaways
  • Show a base case and at least one downside scenario, not a single optimistic projection.
  • Calculate and present Magic Number and Burn Multiple yourself.
  • Break down burn by category: salaries versus other operating expenses.
  • Prepare a considered view on exit strategy and next-round timing.
  • Every number in the model should be traceable to a defensible assumption.
