How to show you can run a bigger operation: leadership and governance

By SheBlooms 7 min read

At Series A, the team question shifts from 'are these good founders' to 'can these people run a much larger operation.' Functional leadership and governance are now part of the answer.

The founders who were sufficient to reach product-market fit are not automatically sufficient to run a fifty-person organization. Series A investors are evaluating whether functional leadership, VP-level roles, and board governance are in place or credibly planned, not whether the founders are still personally capable of every function.

Show functional leadership, not just founders wearing every hat

Name whoever holds, or is being hired for, key functional roles: VP Product, VP Sales, CTO, or their equivalents. If a role is not yet filled, state the specific plan and timeline for filling it, the same way you would for any other unfilled critical gap.

Map functional leadership honestly
List the key functions (product, sales, engineering, finance) and who leads each. If a founder is still covering more than one, name that explicitly rather than implying it is resolved.
2
Address board composition and governance
Investors want to understand who sits on the board, how decisions get made, and whether governance is mature enough for the scale the company is heading toward.
3
Show how the team handles disagreement
A track record of working through hard decisions together is a stronger signal than a team that has never been tested by conflict or a difficult call.
Still founder-dependent
  • Founders personally running product, sales, and engineering
  • No stated plan for VP-level hires
  • Board composition and governance not addressed
Ready to scale
  • Functional leadership named or credibly planned with timelines
  • Board composition and governance explained clearly
  • Evidence the team has navigated real disagreement well
A credible Series A leadership statement
"CTO hired 8 months ago from a 200-person engineering org. VP Sales search underway, targeting close by Q2. Board: 2 founders, 2 investor seats, 1 independent director with prior scaling experience in our sector."
What Series A investors check

Functional leadership in place or credibly planned with timelines. Capacity to run a larger operation, not just the current one. Board composition and governance addressed directly. Advisors with relevant scaling experience.

Key takeaways
  • The team question shifts from 'are these good founders' to 'can they run a larger operation'.
  • Name functional leadership honestly; do not imply a gap is resolved if it is not.
  • Address board composition and governance directly.
  • Show evidence the team has navigated real disagreement, not just harmony.
  • Advisors with relevant scaling experience carry more weight now than general mentors.
