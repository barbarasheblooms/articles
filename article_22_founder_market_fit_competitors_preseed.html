How to map competitors and explain why your team wins

By SheBlooms 6 min read

There is rarely a product moat at Pre-Seed. The differentiation argument leans on founder-market fit: why you, specifically, are the one to build this.

Founders often skip the competitive slide at Pre-Seed, assuming there is nothing to compare against yet. That is a mistake: investors always compare you to something, even if it is an indirect alternative like a spreadsheet, a manual process, or doing nothing at all. Naming that comparison yourself is stronger than letting the investor find it unprompted.

List direct and indirect alternatives, not just competitors

Direct competitors solve the same problem the same way. Indirect alternatives, workarounds, adjacent tools, doing nothing, solve it differently or partially. Both belong on this slide, because customers are choosing between all of them today, not just between you and your closest rival.

1
Name the alternatives customers use today
Think from the customer's perspective: what do they currently do instead of your product, even if it is a manual process or a generic tool not built for this.
2
State why your team wins this specifically
At this stage, the strongest differentiation argument is often founder-market fit: direct experience of the problem, a network others lack, or a skill combination that is hard to replicate.
3
Give one or two sharp differentiators
Resist listing ten reasons you are different. Pick the one or two that actually matter to the customer, and explain briefly why they are hard to copy.
Reads as unconsidered
  • "We have no competitors" or "there is nothing else like us"
  • A long list of differentiators with no clear priority
  • No mention of why the team specifically wins this
Reads as considered
  • Direct and indirect alternatives named specifically
  • One or two sharp, customer-relevant differentiators
  • A clear founder-market fit argument for why your team wins
A credible Pre-Seed competitive statement
"Accelerators take equity and last 3 months. LinkedIn is unstructured. Angel networks are gatekept. Our founder spent 8 years inside this ecosystem building the exact network we now match founders against; we only win when they close."

"Once we stopped saying 'no direct competitors' and started naming the accelerators and angel networks people actually compare us to, the conversation got sharper immediately."

What this slide needs at Pre-Seed

Direct and indirect alternatives named specifically. A founder-market fit argument for why your team is best positioned. One or two clear, customer-relevant points of differentiation, briefly explained.

Key takeaways
  • There is always a comparison, even if it is a manual workaround or doing nothing.
  • List both direct competitors and indirect alternatives customers use today.
  • Lean on founder-market fit as the differentiation argument at this stage.
  • Pick one or two sharp differentiators rather than a long unprioritized list.
  • Explain briefly why your advantage is hard for others to copy.
